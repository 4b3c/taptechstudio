Privacy

Privacy policy.

What we collect, and what we do with it.

TapTech Studio is run by Abram Pierce, a sole proprietor in Arizona. This page says what we collect when you order from us, own a TapTech keychain, or tap one, and what we do with it.

When you order

We keep what you send us to fill the order: your name, email, organization, shipping address, your logo or design files, and the messages about the order. We use them to make, ship and invoice it, and to answer you later. We don't sell them or use them for anything else.

Card payments go through Stripe. We never see or store your card number; Stripe tells us the amount, the date and the name on the payment. Orders placed on Etsy are covered by Etsy's privacy policy as well as this one.

When someone taps a keychain

A tap opens app.taptechstudio.com, which sends the phone on to the keychain's link. For each tap we record:

  • the time, and which keychain was tapped;
  • the phone's browser and operating system, as its browser reports them;
  • an approximate location (country, region and city) worked out by our network provider, Cloudflare, from the connection;
  • the page that sent the request, if the browser reports one.

We don't store the tapper's IP address, and we don't set cookies or run ads or third-party trackers on a tap. The keychain's owner sees tap counts, times and device types for their own keychains, so they can tell how they're being used.

Keychain owners

If you manage your keychains' links, we store the links you set, any scheduled link changes, and a password for your dashboard. Logging in sets one cookie that keeps you signed in for 30 days. It isn't used for anything else.

This website

www.taptechstudio.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare. It has no analytics, no ads and no cookies of its own. GitHub and Cloudflare keep standard server logs, as any web host does.

Who else handles it

Our data is stored with Supabase (the database behind the tap service) and passes through Cloudflare, Stripe and our email provider. Each only gets what it needs to do its job. We share information with anyone else only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it, and your choices

Order records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. Tap records are kept while the keychain is in service. Email us to see, correct or delete what we have about you, and we'll do it unless we're required to keep it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date. Questions go to awpierc2@asu.edu.
